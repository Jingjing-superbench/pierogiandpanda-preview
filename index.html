Will transport be provided?

Guests who book a stay of three nights or more receive complimentary airport pickup. Otherwise, The Slate is approximately a 10-minute taxi ride from Phuket International Airport. We recommend downloading the Grab app, or you can use the airport taxi service on arrival.
